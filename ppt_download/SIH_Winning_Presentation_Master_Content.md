### SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM & OUR VISION

* **Top Header Badge:** `⚡ TEAM: CHEETAH | PS-1736`
* **Slide Title:** EVERY TRANSACTION RESTS ON A SCALE
* **Slide Subtitle:** *"Verification exists in law, but trust is still kept in physical paper files, manual registers, and human memory."*

#### Column 1: The Ground Reality (The Pain Points)
* **Isolated Paper Records:** Over 75M+ commercial scales in India verified via paper slips and physical lead seals, prone to forgery and physical damage.
* **Zero National Visibility:** Central & State directorates lack unified real-time pendency dashboards, leading to unmonitored lapses.
* **Trader Friction:** Honest merchants face sudden penalties due to lack of automated expiry reminders (T-60/30/7 days).
* **Consumer Blindness:** Everyday citizens buying groceries, fuel, or jewelry have no way to verify whether a scale's stamp is genuine or expired.

#### Column 2: Who It Hurts & Why It Matters
* **Consumers (1.4 Billion):** Vulnerable to short-weighing and fraudulent calibrations.
* **Honest Businesses:** Subjected to compliance friction, bureaucratic delays, and arbitrary field audits.
* **Enforcement Officers (LMOs):** Buried under manual record-keeping rather than proactive field inspection.
* **Government of India:** Revenue leakage and lack of national compliance analytics.

#### Bottom Banner: Our Strategic Vision
* **Central Authority Triage:** Intelligent admin dispatch matching applications to local inspectors by district and pincode.
* **One National Digital Trust Layer:** Compliant with Legal Metrology Act 2009, Digital India, and Viksit Bharat 2047.
* **Tagline:** *"From manual paper stamps to real-time tamper-evident QR verification across India."*

### SLIDE 3: TECHNICAL APPROACH & ARCHITECTURE

* **Top Header Badge:** `⚡ TEAM: CHEETAH | PS-1736`
* **Slide Title:** TECHNICAL APPROACH: SECURE, SCALABLE & AUDITABLE
* **Slide Subtitle:** Role-Based Multi-Portal Architecture & Statutory Verification Engine

#### Section A: Core System Principles
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** 4 Dedicated isolated portals:
  1. *HQ Admin Portal:* Central triage, application allocation, national throughput analytics.
  2. *Inspector Portal (LMO):* Mobile-responsive digital workstation with district/pincode matched queues.
  3. *GATC Portal:* Government Approved Test Centers calibration and laboratory batch verification.
  4. *Trader / Business Portal:* Digital registration, auto-location detection, certificate repository.
* **7-Point Statutory Digital Inspection Engine:** Standardized digital inspection workflow covering:
  1. *Visual Condition & Serial Integrity*
  2. *Display Readability & Graduation*
  3. *Zero-Load Return Accuracy*
  4. *Corner Accuracy (Eccentricity Test)*
  5. *Known-Mass Calibration (Repeatability)*
  6. *Physical Stamping & Security Seal Status*
  7. *Operating Environment & Level Compliance*
* **Tamper-Evident QR Verification:** Clean URL verification (`/api/public/verify/:id`) without leaking merchant PII.
* **Immutable Digital Audit Trails:** Cryptographic timestamps and immutable event logs for every application status change.

#### Section B: Dual Architecture Strategy (Prototype vs Scale)
| Component | Working Prototype (Live & Demonstrated) | Scaled National Deployment (Phase 2) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frontend** | React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons | Multi-lingual PWA + Micro-frontends |
| **Backend API** | Node.js, Express.js (REST Engine) | NestJS Microservices + Go Gateways |
| **Database** | MongoDB & Mongoose (Flexible schema) | Distributed PostgreSQL + PostGIS (Geospatial) |
| **Security & Auth**| JWT, bcrypt, HMAC-SHA256 Signatures | Aadhaar/DigiLocker Auth + e-Sign + KMS/HSM |
| **Asynchronous** | In-memory queues & Event Emitters | Redis + BullMQ Background Workers |
| **DevOps** | Monolithic server with health monitoring | Containerized Docker + Kubernetes (K8s) Cluster |
